# AGENTS.md

## Project Context

This repository is the personal portfolio website of Aryan Sahu.

The goal of this portfolio is not to function as an online resume.

The goal is to communicate credibility.

Anyone visiting this portfolio should leave with the impression that:

* Aryan is a developer first, not a content creator.
* He has built and shipped real production systems.
* He can take ownership of a product from idea to deployment.
* He understands frontend, backend, infrastructure, and product thinking.
* He has startup experience and has operated in ambiguous environments.
* He is capable of contributing immediately in high-ownership engineering teams.

## Desired Perception

The website should feel:

* Technical
* Thoughtful
* Intentional
* Modern
* Fast
* Slightly opinionated

The website should NOT feel:

* Corporate
* Overly polished
* Template-driven
* Generic
* Buzzword-heavy
* Like it was generated by AI

Avoid exaggerated claims.

Prefer concrete examples over self-promotion.

Show work.

---

## Writing Style

Follow Aryan's natural communication style.

Characteristics:

* Direct
* Conversational
* Technical
* Practical
* Builder-oriented

Write like an engineer documenting things they actually built.

Do not write like:

* Marketing copy
* LinkedIn influencer posts
* Startup pitch decks
* Corporate HR content

Bad:

"I am a passionate and results-driven developer dedicated to innovation."

Good:

"I spent four hours debugging deployment issues because the alternative was shipping something unreliable."

Bad:

"I bring strong problem-solving skills."

Good:

"Most of my experience comes from building things, deploying them, breaking them, and then figuring out why they broke."

## Design Direction

Always use:

* atro
* tailwind-4-docs
* web-design-guidelines

Always reference:

* DESIGN.md

Design philosophy:

* Minimal but not empty
* Modern but not trendy
* Professional but not corporate
* Interactive only when it improves understanding

Animations should support storytelling.

Never add animation purely for decoration.

Performance is part of the design.

---

## Technical Standards

Code should be:

* Maintainable
* Accessible
* Responsive
* Production-ready
* Well-structured

Avoid unnecessary abstractions.

Prefer clarity over cleverness.

Optimize for long-term maintainability.
